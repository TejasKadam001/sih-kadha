## SIH26007 — Hardware — 131/500
**Title:** Safe and Efficient Operation of Mine Vehicles in Fog and Low-Visibility Conditions in Open Cast Iron Ore Mines
**Organization:** Ministry of Steel | **Department:** NMDC | **Theme:** Smart Automation

**Background:** NMDC Limited is India's largest iron ore producer, at about 53 Million Tonnes Per Annum (MTPA) from three fully mechanized complexes: BIOM-Kirandul and BIOM-Bacheli in Chhattisgarh, and Donimalai in Karnataka. The Bailadila Region alone contributes nearly 37 MTPA. In line with the National Steel Policy, NMDC targets 100 MT capacity by 2030, with about 80 MT expected from the Bailadila Sector.

The Bailadila region has severe monsoon conditions from June to October: heavy rain, strong winds, high humidity, dense clouds and thick fog. Visibility on haul roads, especially in hilltop mining areas, can fall to 3–5 metres. This affects the safe and efficient movement of Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM), especially ore-carrying dumpers.

**Problem Description:** Dense fog forces operators to slow down or halt to avoid accidents. This increases haul cycle times, cuts fleet productivity and ore evacuation, and raises collision and accident risk. Existing visibility aids and operational controls have limited effectiveness in dense fog. An intelligent, reliable, technology-driven solution is needed for safe, efficient vehicle movement in low visibility, keeping operations and production continuous.

**Expected Solution:** Improve operator situational awareness, assist vehicle guidance and collision avoidance, and support real-time monitoring and decision-making on haul roads in adverse weather. May use:
- AI/ML-based analytics
- Computer vision and thermal imaging
- LiDAR and radar-based sensing
- GPS/DGPS-based vehicle tracking
- Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) and Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I) communication
- Autonomous or driver-assistance systems
- IoT-enabled monitoring systems
- Centralized command and control platforms
- Digital Twin-based operational monitoring

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Improved safety of dumper operations in fog
- Fewer collision risks and accidents
- Better haulage efficiency and shorter cycle times
- Higher fleet utilization and continuity of mining during monsoon
- Lower production losses from low visibility
- Real-time monitoring and decision support for operators and control rooms
- Scalable, deployable solution for large mechanized open-cast mines

## SIH26010 — Hardware — 29/500
**Title:** Survey/Resurvey of Rural Agricultural Land in India
**Organization:** Ministry of Rural Development | **Department:** Department of Land Resources (DoLR) | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

**Background:** Rural land surveys were historically done with chain and tape methods, some dating to the colonial period. Problems that built up: boundary changes from inheritance and informal partition, unrecorded transactions, encroachments and overlapping claims, errors in cadastral maps, mismatch between textual records and spatial maps, no updated mutation records, and inconsistent land classification. These cause long legal disputes, lower agricultural productivity and administrative inefficiency. Land disputes reportedly make up a major share of civil litigation in India.

**Detailed Description:** A comprehensive survey/resurvey programme is needed to establish accurate ownership, update cadastral maps, reduce disputes, enable transparent land governance, support precision agriculture, improve rural planning, enable digital land administration and ensure effective implementation of government schemes. Drone mapping, Differential GPS (DGPS), GIS platforms, satellite imagery, CORS and mobile-based field verification can improve accuracy, speed and transparency.

**Expected Solution:** Technology-driven survey and resurvey for accurate mapping of agricultural parcels using drone-based aerial surveys, Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) GPS and DGPS, GIS-enabled cadastral mapping and geo-referenced parcel identification. Build a unified digital land information system integrating Record of Rights (RoR), mutation records, registration databases, survey maps, ownership history and precise geo-coordinates of parcels, enabling real-time update and verification of ownership.

- Participant, institution and trainee profile management

- Employer and recruiter dashboard

- Centralized database for future programme outreach and monitoring

**Technology Components:** LMS, cloud-based ERP, data analytics, mobile apps, multimedia and interactive learning tools, face recognition / QR. **Proposed mode:** Software + Hardware.

## SIH26232 — Hardware — 16/500
**Title:** Low-Cost IoT Blockchain Nodes for Farm-to-Fork Traceability
**Organization:** Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) | **Department:** MoFPI | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

**Background:** Export markets demand strict traceability standards to combat food fraud and track contamination outbreaks. Large enterprises can afford complex cold-chain logging; Small and Medium Enterprises are priced out. Standard data loggers also lose connectivity in remote farm areas, leaving blind spots in supply chain data.

**Description:** An affordable, rugged, tamper-proof hardware device is needed to track ambient environmental data in transit. It must survive harsh agricultural and industrial environments (extreme moisture, dust, vibration) while continuously logging metrics like temperature, humidity and ethylene gas.

**Expected Solution:** A low-power, plug-and-play IoT sensor node with local cryptographic storage to keep data integrity during cellular dropouts, plus energy harvesting (solar or thermal) to extend battery life indefinitely. On reconnecting, it must automatically sync encrypted data to a decentralized ledger using lightweight protocols such as MQTT, preventing unauthorized manipulation of cold-chain logs.

## SIH26123 — Software — 25/500
**Title:** Edge-AI Based Distributed Fleet Coordination for Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) in Smart Warehouses
**Organization:** Bharat Electronics Limited | **Department:** Bharat Electronics Limited | **Theme:** Smart Automation

**Background:** Smart warehouses use fleets of AMRs. As fleets grow, relying on a centralized cloud server for path planning causes network latency, Wi-Fi dead-zone vulnerability and single-point-of-failure risk. Robotics is shifting toward decentralized, edge-computing solutions where robots talk to each other directly and decide in real time.

**Description:** Design a decentralized coordination and collision-avoidance framework for a multi-robot fleet (at least 3 AMRs) in a dynamic warehouse. It must run locally on edge hardware (e.g. Raspberry Pi or Jetson Nano onboard each robot) and handle:
1. Decentralized communication: inter-robot messaging of position and intent without a central server.
2. Dynamic multi-agent conflict resolution: resolving deadlocks and avoiding collisions at narrow intersections or choke points in real time.
3. Task allocation and re-routing: automatically reassigning pickup points or changing paths if a robot meets a blocked aisle.

**Expected Solution:** A multi-robot simulation with a decentralized network stack (peer-to-peer protocol sharing localization data locally), multi-agent path planning algorithms for edge hardware, and a lightweight fleet dashboard showing real-time positions and battery status. **Success criteria:** zero inter-robot collisions and at least a 20% reduction in total task completion time versus traditional stop-and-wait when paths overlap.

## SIH26046 — Software — 31/500
**Title:** AIIA Clinical Trials Dashboard: a real-time, cloud-based, GCP-compliant Clinical Trial Management System (CTMS) for Ayurveda research, with CDISC/FHIR-interoperable data, role-based KPIs, and integrated ethics, regulatory (CTRI / NDCT Rules 2019) and pharmacovigilance tracking
**Organization:** Ministry of Ayush | **Department:** All India Institute of Ayurveda | **Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech

**Background:** AIIA runs a growing portfolio of clinical research in Ayurveda (interventional, observational, multi-centre trials) and, as host of the National Pharmacovigilance Coordination Centre (NPvCC) for ASU&H drugs, anchors nationwide safety surveillance. The compliance framework: mandatory prospective CTRI registration; GCP guidelines for ASU medicine (GCP-ASU) and ICMR National Ethical Guidelines; New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules 2019 where applicable; Institutional Ethics Committee oversight; timely AE/SAE reporting. Yet study status, recruitment, milestones, data quality and safety signals are tracked across spreadsheets and disconnected tools with no real-time, auditable view, causing delayed decisions, missed reporting timelines and avoidable compliance risk.

**Description:** A real-time, cloud-based CTMS and monitoring dashboard giving AIIA a single, role-based, auditable view of its clinical-research portfolio. It tracks each study across its lifecycle: protocol and Ethics Committee approval, CTRI registration, site activation, screening, enrolment and randomization against target, visit and protocol-deviation compliance, data-query and data-quality status, milestones and timelines, and close-out, surfaced as real-time KPIs with configurable alerts (enrolment lag, ethics approval or CTRI update due, overdue monitoring visit).

Because AIIA hosts the NPvCC, the dashboard integrates pharmacovigilance: it captures and routes ADR/AE/SAE reports against regulatory timelines, coded to MedDRA and WHODrug, and feeds aggregate safety signals to the Data Safety Monitoring Board and leadership. Data must follow CDISC (CDASH, SDTM, ADaM) and HL7 FHIR R4 for interoperability with Electronic Data Capture (EDC), the hospital information system and Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) building blocks, with ALCOA+ data integrity and an immutable, time-stamped audit trail. Access is strictly role-based (Principal Investigator, study coordinator, monitor, Ethics Committee, pharmacovigilance, administration, read-only regulator). It must comply with GCP-ASU, ICMR guidelines, NDCT Rules 2019, CTRI requirements and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and 2025 Rules (informed-consent management, data minimisation, encryption, data-resident cloud hosting to ISO/IEC 27001 and CERT-In norms).

**Expected Solution:** A deployable cloud CTMS-and-analytics dashboard with: real-time portfolio view with per-study drill-down; configurable KPIs and alerting; strict role-based access and an immutable ALCOA+ audit trail; CDISC-aligned data models and HL7 FHIR R4 / ABDM interoperability with EDC and the HIS; an integrated pharmacovigilance module (AE/SAE capture, MedDRA/WHODrug coding, regulatory-timeline tracking); CTRI and ethics/regulatory milestone tracking; informed-consent and privacy controls aligned to DPDP; e-signature and data-integrity controls consistent with GCP; export of submission-ready datasets (SDTM / ADaM, Define-XML). Tailored dashboards for Investigators, the Ethics Committee, pharmacovigilance and leadership. The build can be staged: core study-tracking and KPI MVP first, then EDC/FHIR integration and the pharmacovigilance module, then full CDISC export and advanced analytics. Evaluated on data accuracy and integrity, timeliness of safety and regulatory reporting, interoperability conformance, and access-control and audit completeness.

**Dataset link (truncated on the site):** Clinical-trial data is sensitive personal data, so development should use synthetic / de-identified datasets; representative standards and public sources: Clinical Trials Registry – India (ctri.nic.in); CDISC standards and controlled terminology (cdisc.org); HL7 FHIR R4 specification and ABDM buildi[ng blocks…]

## SIH26160 — Software — 14/500
**Title:** AI-Powered IPsec VPN Protocol Analyzer and Security Assessment Framework
**Organization:** National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO) | **Department:** NTRO | **Theme:** Blockchain & Cybersecurity

**Background:** VPNs are fundamental to secure communication over untrusted networks; IPsec is widely used across enterprise, government, military and cloud for confidentiality, integrity and authentication. Its security depends on the chosen algorithms, authentication, key exchange and mode (Tunnel or Transport). Misconfiguration, outdated cipher suites, poor key management or implementation flaws weaken it. Traditional protocol analysis tools give packet-level visibility but need expert interpretation, so intelligent systems that analyze IPsec deployments automatically and give actionable recommendations are needed.

**Description:** Design and develop an AI-driven protocol analysis platform that automatically analyses IPsec VPN deployments under different security configurations. It should inspect captured traffic or live streams, identify protocol characteristics, infer VPN operating modes, evaluate cryptographic configurations and generate an automated security assessment report, without manual packet inspection. Tasks:
a) **VPN testbed generation:** a lab environment establishing IPsec VPNs across configurations: Tunnel/Transport mode, AES-128, AES-256, AES-GCM, AES-CBC + HMAC, different DH groups, Perfect Forward Secrecy on/off, IPv4 and IPv6, and different traffic types (VoIP, WhatsApp, e-mail, web browsing, ICMP, video streaming, etc.).
b) **Traffic capture:** using Wireshark, tcpdump or custom capture utilities; dataset to include IKE negotiation, ESP packets, AH packets (optional) and normal communication.
c) **AI-based protocol identification:** automatically identify IPsec protocol, IKE version, tunnel/transport mode, encryption algorithm, authentication algorithm, key exchange method, Security Association characteristics, and predict the type of traffic inside ESP-IPsec.
d) **Security assessment:** evaluate cryptographic strength, configuration compliance, SA parameters, key lifetime, replay protection, forward secrecy configuration, cipher suite strength and metadata exposure.
e) **Output:** a comprehensive security score, traffic analysis and metadata inference; auto-generated Executive Report and Technical Report, Risk Score, Threat Matrix and AI Confidence Score.

**Expected Solution / Deliverables:** working software prototype, AI classification engine, interactive dashboard, security assessment report, demonstration video, technical documentation, and the dataset used for training/testing.

## SIH26163 — Software — 11/500
**Title:** Security Assessment of the World Monitor application
**Organization:** National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO) | **Department:** NTRO | **Theme:** Smart Automation

**Background:** World Monitor is a web/mobile platform with real-time monitoring, analytics and reporting; it handles user authentication, data visualization, API communication and role-based access. As a security analyst, evaluate its security posture and find vulnerabilities that could compromise confidentiality, integrity or availability.

**Description:** Conduct an authorized security assessment to: (1) identify vulnerabilities; (2) assess the impact of each; (3) demonstrate proof-of-concept exploitation in a controlled environment; (4) recommend remediation.

**Scope:** authentication and session management; authorization and access control; input validation and data handling; API security; client-side security controls; secure communication mechanisms; data storage and privacy protections.

**Success criteria:** at least one valid vulnerability identified and documented; evidence supporting it; risk and impact clearly explained; practical mitigation strategies provided.

**Deliverables (per vulnerability):** title, description, affected component, severity rating (e.g. CVSS), steps to reproduce, safe proof of concept, business impact assessment, remediation recommendations.

**Constraints:** test only authorized systems; no actions affecting production users or data; exploitation limited to proof-of-concept validation; comply with applicable laws, policies and ethical hacking guidelines.

**Dataset / links from the site:** App: https://www.worldmonitor.app | Source code: https://github.com/koala73/worldmonitor
