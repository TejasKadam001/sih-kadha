Meet iKwath — The Smart Countertop Kadha Maker

Kadha is meant to be brewed fresh and drank warm, not preserved in plastic bottles. iKwath gently soaks the herbs, simmers at low heat (85–90°C), boils down to the right strength, and pours a clean cup in roughly 12 minutes.

01

Gentle Heat (85–90°C)

Ayurvedic herbs lose their healing power when boiled aggressively. iKwath keeps the temperature strictly below 90°C, protecting delicate plant nutrients while extracting full herbal strength.

02

Fast Boil with Vacuum Tech

Traditional boiling takes 45 to 60 minutes. Our vacuum chamber lowers boiling pressure so excess water evaporates fast at gentle heat. It reduces 400 mL down to a potent 100 mL cup in roughly 12 minutes.

03

Pre-Measured, Mess-Free Pods

Each pod contains the exact herb ratio with a built-in micro filter. No messy loose powders, no residue in your cup, and 100% verified pure herbs.

04

Automatic Strength Check

Smart internal sensors monitor liquid weight and herbal density live, ensuring your cup reaches the exact traditional strength before pouring.

05

Continuous Herb Circulation

A gentle internal pump continuously circulates hot water through the herbs, extracting every drop of goodness evenly without burning.

06

Triple Safety Protection

Safety built into the hardware: heat cut-off switches, child-safe lid locks, and electric surge protectors prevent accidents automatically.
